## Performance Results

### Cross-Validation (Training Set Only)

**Best CV Score:** 0.9404 (94.04% macro-F1)

**Best Hyperparameters:**
- `n_estimators`: 100 trees
- `criterion`: gini (impurity measure)
- `max_depth`: 20 (maximum tree depth)
- `max_features`: sqrt (features per split)
- `min_samples_leaf`: 2 (minimum samples in leaf)
- `class_weight`: None (no reweighting)

**Training Details:**
- Training samples: 47,696
- Features selected: 9 out of 21
- Cross-validation: 5-fold stratified
- Scoring: f1_macro
- Training time: 99.13 seconds (~1.6 minutes)

**Selected Features (9):**
1. duration
2. max_flowiat
3. std_flowiat
4. mean_fiat
5. mean_flowiat
6. flowBytesPerSecond
7. std_fiat
8. max_fiat
9. mean_biat

**Feature Importance Ranking:**
1. **duration** (14.26%) - Flow duration in seconds
2. **max_flowiat** (13.55%) - Maximum inter-arrival time between flow packets
3. **std_flowiat** (12.29%) - Standard deviation of flow IAT
4. **mean_fiat** (11.33%) - Mean forward (client→server) IAT
5. **mean_flowiat** (10.52%) - Mean flow IAT
6. **flowBytesPerSecond** (9.89%) - Throughput
7. **std_fiat** (9.55%) - Std dev of forward IAT
8. **max_fiat** (9.38%) - Max forward IAT
9. **mean_biat** (9.23%) - Mean backward (server→client) IAT

**Key Insights:**
- **Timing features dominate:** 8 of 9 features are timing-related (IAT patterns)
- **Duration most important:** How long a flow lasts distinguishes traffic types
- **Directional patterns matter:** Forward and backward IAT separately tracked
- **Volume feature included:** flowBytesPerSecond (throughput) is 6th most important
