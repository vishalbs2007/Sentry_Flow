## Known Limitations

1. **Low CV Score:** 65.55% macro-F1 is significantly lower than Decision Tree (94.38%) and Random Forest (94.04%). Possible reasons:
   - **Subsampling impact:** Training on 42% of data reduces learning potential
   - **Feature/kernel mismatch:** RBF kernel may not suit flow-statistic feature patterns
   - **Hyperparameter space:** Limited search space due to time constraints
   - **Class imbalance:** Even with balanced weights, SVM sensitive to imbalance

2. **Training on Subsample:** SVM trained on 20,012 samples while other models trained on 47,696. This is NOT a fair comparison but is disclosed per RID-06.

3. **Search Space Limited:** Only 24 combinations tested (C[3] × kernel[2] × gamma[2] × class_weight[2]) vs potentially larger space.

4. **No Probability Estimates:** `probability=False` means no `predict_proba()` available. Would require expensive Platt scaling.

5. **RBF Kernel May Not Be Best:** LinearSVC might perform better and faster, but was not tested separately.

6. **Performance Gap Unexpected:** The 30-point gap from tree methods suggests SVM may not be well-suited for this dataset/feature set.

## Analysis: Why Is SVM Performance Low?

### Hypothesis 1: Subsampling Impact
- Trained on 20k samples vs 48k for other models
- Kernel methods may need more data to learn complex boundaries
- **Counter:** Even with subsample, 20k is substantial

### Hypothesis 2: Feature/Kernel Mismatch
- Flow statistics may have linear or simple non-linear patterns
- RBF kernel assumes complex non-linear boundaries
- Tree methods (piecewise constant) may better suit feature types
- **Evidence:** Decision Tree (single tree) outperforms SVM by 29 points

### Hypothesis 3: Hyperparameter Space Insufficient
- Only tested C[0.1, 1.0, 10.0] and gamma['scale', 'auto']
- May need wider C range or custom gamma values
- **Note:** Time constraints prevented extensive search

### Hypothesis 4: Class Imbalance Sensitivity
- Streaming class only 4.6% of data
- Even with class_weight='balanced', SVM may struggle
- Tree methods handle imbalance better with splits

### Hypothesis 5: Feature Scaling Not Enough
- MinMaxScaler applied (features in [0,1])
- But original feature distributions may be heavily skewed
- StandardScaler might work better for SVM

**Recommended Follow-up (if time permits):**
1. Try LinearSVC (faster, may work better)
2. Try StandardScaler instead of MinMaxScaler
3. Expand hyperparameter search (wider C range)
4. Train on full dataset if time allows (no subsample)

**For Now:** Accept result and proceed. Test set evaluation (CN-010) will provide definitive comparison.
